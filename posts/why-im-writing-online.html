Why I’m writing online

Oct 27, 2026

  • I want to be a better thinker (and spend more time thinking), and the standard advice is to write
  • By default I consume a lot but create basically nothing
  • The people I admire write publicly
  • I enjoy reading other people's blogs
  • My professional reputation to date (IB, PE) rests on a few people's references, and I want something that stands on its own
